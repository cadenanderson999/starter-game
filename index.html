How to play
Pick a building and click the map to place it. Villagers arrive when you have housing and go to work on their own. Keep them fed and amused.

At night, mobs raid from the edges of the map and smash whatever they reach. Walls slow them down, towers shoot them, soldiers and your Mayor 🤠 fight them. Click the ground to move the Mayor, click a mob to attack it.

Lose the Town Hall and the town falls.

🏘️ Hollowmere

Build a town by day. Survive the raids by night.

You start with a Town Hall, two villagers, and a small pile of wood. Build houses so villagers move in, farms so they eat, mills and mines for materials. Villagers have needs — hunger, energy and fun — and happy villagers work faster.

Every night mobs 👺👹🧌 come to wreck your town. Fortify with walls, archer towers, barracks, and lead the fight yourself as the Mayor 🤠.

1–8 pick a building · Esc cancel · Space pause · X demolish · R repair

Runs completely offline. Progress is saved in this browser automatically.
