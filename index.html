How to play
WASD walk the Mayor around, Shift to dash. The camera follows.
Pick a building and click the map to place it. Hold and drag to paint a line of walls. Chop trees with Demolish for wood.

Villagers arrive when you have housing and go to work on their own. Keep them fed and amused.

At night, mobs raid from the edges of the forest. Walls slow them down, towers shoot them, soldiers fight them, and you left-click to shoot. Q is your War Cry. Right-click anything to inspect it.

Lose the Town Hall and the town falls.

🏘️ Hollowmere

Build a town by day. Survive the raids by night.

You start with a Town Hall, two villagers, and a small pile of wood. Build houses so villagers move in, farms so they eat, mills and mines for materials. Villagers have needs — hunger, energy and fun — and happy villagers work faster.

Every night goblins, orcs, trolls, shamans and bombers come out of the forest — and every fifth night the Troll King himself — to wreck your town. Fortify with walls, archer towers and barracks, and lead the fight yourself as the Mayor 🤠 — walk with WASD, shoot with the mouse.

WASD move · left-click shoot / place · right-click inspect · 1–8 pick a building · Esc cancel · Space pause · X demolish / chop · R repair · Q War Cry · Shift dash · M mute
